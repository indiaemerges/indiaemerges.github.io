About

indiaemerges is an independent data journalism project about how India is building its economy: where growth comes from, how governments raise and spend money, what the country makes, trades and imports, and what that means for the people who live there.

Its dashboards let anyone explore the numbers behind the headlines, from state budgets to industrial output. Its stories, published as the Forging India newsletter, follow one question at a time, such as why India imports fertiliser, or how differently its states govern themselves, and answer it with data.

The project is unaffiliated with any government, party or company, and is free to read.

Data come from the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI), the Reserve Bank of India, the Census of India, NITI Aayog, PPAC, the agriculture ministry, FCI, IMD and the World Bank.

  • Official sourcesEvery figure comes from a government or multilateral release, not from secondary aggregators.
  • Exact valuesNumbers are taken exactly as published. When a value is missing, the chart says so instead of filling the gap.
  • Sources on every chartEach dashboard names its source and explains how derived measures, such as ratios to GSDP or constant prices, are calculated.
  • Open codeThe dashboards are open source on GitHub. Corrections and ideas are welcome there.
